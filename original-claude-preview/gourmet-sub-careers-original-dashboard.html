Fair hiring

Hiring, done right.

Equal opportunity

We hire on skills, attitude and availability. We never ask about age, gender, ethnicity, religion, family status, disability or health in an application, in line with the Human Rights Act 1993.

Adjustments, on request

Tell us what would help you at interview and we’ll arrange it.

Right to work

Employers in New Zealand must check that every new hire can legally work here. We’ll ask you to confirm it.

A written agreement

You’ll receive your employment agreement, and time to read it and get advice, before you sign.

Your privacy

We collect only what we need and keep it only as long as we need it. Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct it at any time.

Gourmet Sub is an equal opportunity employer. Pay rates are subject to the minimum wage in force at the time. Role details are a summary only; your employment agreement sets out the full terms.
